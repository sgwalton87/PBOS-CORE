Authority

This artifact derives its authority from the PBOS Constitution.

It establishes the constitutional compliance framework governing every activity performed within PBOS.

---

Purpose

Constitutional compliance ensures that every engineering activity remains aligned with constitutional authority.

Compliance is not optional.

Compliance is a prerequisite for trust.

Compliance shall be continuously demonstrated through measurable engineering evidence.

---

Constitutional Compliance

Every governed artifact shall demonstrate compliance with:

constitutional authority;

founder intent;

documented specifications;

approved architecture;

engineering standards;

security requirements;

validation requirements;

certification requirements.

Compliance shall exist before execution, during execution, and after execution.

---

Compliance Requirements

Compliance shall be:

continuous;

deterministic;

auditable;

explainable;

traceable;

measurable;

repeatable.

Evidence shall accompany every compliance assertion.

---

Compliance Evidence

PBOS shall preserve evidence demonstrating:

constitutional lineage;

engineering ownership;

decision rationale;

implementation integrity;

validation outcomes;

certification history;

repository evolution.

Compliance without evidence shall not constitute compliance.

---

Non-Compliance

Engineering activities that violate constitutional authority shall fail closed.

PBOS shall prevent certification of non-compliant engineering.

Exceptions shall require constitutional authorization.

---

PBOS Responsibilities

PBOS shall continuously monitor constitutional compliance.

PBOS shall prevent constitutional drift.

PBOS shall preserve permanent compliance evidence.

PBOS shall continuously improve compliance through governed evolution.

---

Validation

Compliance shall be independently validated before certification.

No engineering artifact shall self-certify constitutional compliance.

---

Definition of Done

Constitutional Compliance is complete when every engineering activity possesses verifiable evidence demonstrating continuous constitutional conformity.
