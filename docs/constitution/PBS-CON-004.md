Authority

This artifact derives its authority from the PBOS Constitution.

It establishes the constitutional rights protected by PBOS.

---

Purpose

Rights protect participants within PBOS from arbitrary engineering behavior while preserving constitutional governance.

Rights define what may not be infringed.

---

Constitutional Rights

Founders possess the right to define vision, purpose, mission, and strategic direction.

Engineering teams possess the right to transparent governance, documented standards, and deterministic engineering processes.

Users possess the right to secure, reliable, and governed software.

Artificial intelligence possesses no constitutional rights independent of constitutional authority.

PBOS itself possesses the responsibility to preserve constitutional integrity rather than personal interests.

---

Protection of Rights

Rights shall be preserved through governance.

Rights shall not be diminished through implementation.

Rights shall survive technological change.

Rights shall remain protected throughout repository evolution.

---

Engineering Rights

Every engineering artifact possesses the right to:

constitutional lineage;

documented ownership;

version history;

traceable dependencies;

validation evidence;

certification evidence;

and permanent engineering history.

---

Validation

PBOS shall continuously verify that constitutional rights remain protected throughout planning, engineering, validation, certification, release, and operations.

---

Definition of Done

Constitutional Rights are complete when every governed participant and engineering artifact receives the protections established by this Constitution.
