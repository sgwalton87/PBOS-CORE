Authority

This document derives its authority from the PBOS Constitution.

It establishes the constitutional inheritance model governing every artifact created within PBOS.

---

Purpose

The purpose of this document is to ensure that every engineering artifact derives explicit authority from higher-order constitutional artifacts.

Inheritance establishes deterministic governance, eliminates ambiguity, and preserves institutional integrity.

---

Inheritance Philosophy

Nothing exists independently.

Every artifact exists because a higher constitutional authority permits its existence.

Inheritance defines legitimacy.

Dependencies define implementation.

These relationships are distinct.

---

Constitutional Lineage

Every artifact shall explicitly declare:

its constitutional authority;

its engineering dependencies;

its owner;

its governing mission;

its validation requirements; and

its certification requirements.

PBOS shall continuously preserve this lineage.

---

Inheritance Hierarchy

Constitution.

Vision.

Charter.

Specifications.

Architecture.

Engineering.

Implementation.

Validation.

Certification.

Release.

Each successive layer inherits authority from every preceding layer.

Inheritance is cumulative.

Inheritance may be extended but shall never be weakened.

---

Inheritance Rules

Every canonical artifact shall:

identify constitutional authority;

inherit governance;

preserve lineage;

maintain traceability;

support deterministic reasoning;

remain independently versioned.

Artifacts lacking constitutional inheritance shall fail validation.

---

PBOS Responsibilities

PBOS shall:

construct inheritance graphs;

validate inheritance chains;

detect inheritance violations;

prevent orphaned artifacts;

maintain constitutional lineage;

and preserve engineering integrity throughout repository evolution.

---

Validation Rules

PBOS shall continuously verify that every artifact possesses complete constitutional inheritance.

Missing, invalid, or conflicting inheritance shall prevent certification.

Inheritance violations shall fail closed.

---

Definition of Done

Constitutional inheritance is complete when every canonical artifact within PBOS possesses a valid, traceable, deterministic inheritance chain terminating at PBS-CON-000.

---

Future Evolution

Additional inheritance mechanisms may be introduced provided they preserve constitutional authority, backward compatibility, and deterministic engineering.
