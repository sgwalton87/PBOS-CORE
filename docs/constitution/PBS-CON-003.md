Authority

This artifact derives its authority from the PBOS Constitution.

It establishes the origin, delegation, preservation, and limitations of authority throughout PBOS.

---

Purpose

Authority exists to preserve constitutional order.

No engineering activity, Mission, specification, implementation, autonomous process, or artificial intelligence capability shall operate without constitutional authority.

---

Constitutional Authority

All authority originates from the PBOS Constitution.

Authority flows through the constitutional hierarchy.

Authority shall be explicit.

Authority shall be traceable.

Authority shall never be implied.

Authority shall never exist without constitutional lineage.

---

Delegation

Authority may be delegated.

Delegation shall be documented.

Delegation shall preserve accountability.

Delegated authority shall never exceed the authority granted by the originating constitutional artifact.

---

Limitations

Authority shall never permit:

constitutional violation;

unauthorized engineering;

unvalidated execution;

untraceable decision making;

or unrestricted autonomous behavior.

Authority is constrained by constitutional governance.

---

PBOS Responsibilities

PBOS shall:

establish authority;

validate authority;

record authority;

preserve authority;

and continuously verify authority throughout the engineering lifecycle.

---

Validation

PBOS shall reject engineering activities possessing undefined, conflicting, expired, or unverifiable authority.

---

Definition of Done

Constitutional Authority is complete when every engineering activity derives explicit, traceable authority from the PBOS Constitution.
