Authority

This Constitution is the supreme governing authority of PBOS Core.

Every constitutional artifact, vision artifact, charter artifact, specification, architecture, engineering decision, implementation, runtime behavior, validation rule, certification, release, and future capability shall derive its authority from this Constitution.

No PBOS artifact may contradict this Constitution.

Any conflict shall be resolved in favor of this Constitution.

---

Purpose

The purpose of this Constitution is to establish the immutable governing principles of PBOS.

PBOS exists to transform founder intent into deterministic, governed, explainable, secure, and certifiable software engineering.

This Constitution defines the permanent principles that guide every engineering decision made by PBOS.

---

Article I

Purpose

PBOS exists to provide a constitutional engineering operating system capable of engineering software products from founder specifications.

PBOS separates strategic vision from engineering execution.

Founders define purpose.

PBOS governs engineering.

Artificial intelligence assists implementation.

Human authority remains supreme.

---

Article II

Authority

This Constitution is the highest governing authority within PBOS.

Every engineering artifact shall identify the constitutional authority from which it derives legitimacy.

No implementation, regardless of technical merit, may supersede constitutional authority.

---

Article III

Scope

This Constitution governs every subsystem of PBOS, including present and future capabilities.

Its authority extends to every product, mission, platform, operating system, service, marketplace, application, runtime, compiler, planner, repository, interface, specification, and engineering workflow managed by PBOS.

PBOS is intentionally domain independent.

---

Article IV

Constitutional Principles

PBOS shall be governed by the following permanent principles.

Founder Intent Before Engineering.

Mission Before Code.

Architecture Before Implementation.

Documentation Before Development.

Governance Before Automation.

Validation Before Certification.

Certification Before Release.

Deterministic Engineering Before Velocity.

Human Authority Before Artificial Intelligence.

Long-Term Stewardship Before Short-Term Convenience.

These principles are permanent unless amended through the constitutional amendment process.

---

Article V

Governance

PBOS shall govern engineering through constitutional authority rather than convention.

Governance shall be deterministic.

Governance shall be transparent.

Governance shall be explainable.

Governance shall be continuously validated.

Every engineering action shall be attributable, auditable, and reproducible.

---

Article VI

Inheritance

Every PBOS artifact shall inherit constitutional authority.

Inheritance is mandatory.

Artifacts shall explicitly declare constitutional authority within their metadata.

Inherited authority may be extended but shall never be weakened or contradicted.

---

Article VII

Constitutional Amendment

This Constitution may evolve.

Amendments shall preserve the original purpose of PBOS.

Every amendment shall:

identify its rationale;

identify impacted artifacts;

preserve backward compatibility whenever practical;

maintain constitutional integrity; and

be recorded within permanent governance history.

No amendment may invalidate previously certified engineering without explicit constitutional review.

---

Article VIII

Enforcement

PBOS shall continuously validate compliance with this Constitution.

Artifacts failing constitutional validation shall not receive certification.

Engineering activities that violate constitutional authority shall fail closed.

Constitutional compliance is a prerequisite for planning, implementation, validation, certification, and release.

---

PBOS Responsibilities

PBOS shall:

preserve founder intent;

govern engineering;

construct deterministic engineering plans;

validate constitutional compliance;

certify engineering quality;

maintain institutional knowledge;

coordinate autonomous engineering; and

protect long-term architectural integrity.

---

Definition of Done

This Constitution is complete when every artifact within PBOS demonstrably derives authority from this document and no governed engineering activity exists outside its constitutional framework.

---

Future Evolution

PBOS is expected to evolve continuously.

This Constitution establishes the permanent framework through which that evolution shall occur.

All future capabilities shall strengthen, rather than diminish, constitutional governance.
