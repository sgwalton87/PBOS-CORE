Authority

This artifact derives its authority from the PBOS Constitution.

It establishes the permanent constitutional responsibilities governing PBOS.

---

Purpose

Responsibilities establish what every governed participant shall uphold in order to preserve constitutional engineering.

Rights and responsibilities shall remain inseparable.

---

Founder Responsibilities

Founders shall define:

vision;

mission;

purpose;

organizational values;

business objectives;

and strategic priorities.

Founders shall preserve constitutional integrity throughout product evolution.

---

PBOS Responsibilities

PBOS shall:

govern engineering;

preserve founder intent;

maintain constitutional lineage;

coordinate autonomous engineering;

validate engineering quality;

certify governed engineering outcomes;

protect institutional knowledge;

and continuously improve the engineering operating system.

---

Engineering Responsibilities

Engineering shall:

operate constitutionally;

maintain deterministic behavior;

preserve traceability;

produce measurable evidence;

document rationale;

and support long-term maintainability.

---

Artificial Intelligence Responsibilities

Artificial intelligence shall:

operate only within delegated constitutional authority;

preserve transparency;

support engineering;

produce explainable outputs;

maintain engineering evidence;

and remain continuously subject to constitutional validation.

---

Validation

PBOS shall continuously verify fulfillment of constitutional responsibilities.

Failure to satisfy constitutional responsibilities shall prevent certification.

---

Definition of Done

Constitutional Responsibilities are complete when every governed participant possesses explicit, measurable, enforceable responsibilities that preserve constitutional engineering.
