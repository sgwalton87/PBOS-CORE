Authority

This artifact derives its authority from the PBOS Constitution.

It establishes the constitutional process through which PBOS evolves while preserving constitutional integrity.

---

Purpose

PBOS is designed to evolve indefinitely.

Evolution shall strengthen the engineering operating system without compromising constitutional authority.

Innovation shall occur within governance.

Progress shall preserve continuity.

---

Evolution Philosophy

Constitutional stability enables engineering innovation.

The Constitution shall evolve deliberately.

Engineering may evolve continuously.

Technologies may change.

Constitutional principles shall remain stable unless formally amended.

---

Evolution Principles

Every constitutional evolution shall:

preserve founder intent;

preserve engineering lineage;

maintain deterministic behavior;

protect backward compatibility whenever practical;

improve engineering quality;

strengthen governance;

reduce ambiguity.

---

Amendment Requirements

Every constitutional amendment shall document:

purpose;

rationale;

scope;

affected artifacts;

migration strategy;

validation requirements;

expected outcomes.

Every amendment shall preserve historical traceability.

---

Evolution Governance

No constitutional amendment shall invalidate previously certified engineering without explicit constitutional review.

Deprecated constitutional artifacts shall remain permanently discoverable.

Historical constitutional lineage shall never be destroyed.

---

PBOS Responsibilities

PBOS shall preserve constitutional history.

PBOS shall govern constitutional evolution.

PBOS shall validate amendment compatibility.

PBOS shall maintain complete constitutional lineage across every version of PBOS.

---

Validation

Every constitutional amendment shall undergo constitutional validation before becoming effective.

Unvalidated amendments shall possess no constitutional authority.

---

Definition of Done

Constitutional Evolution is complete when PBOS continuously improves while preserving constitutional integrity, engineering continuity, and institutional knowledge.
