Authority

This artifact derives its authority from the PBOS Constitution.

It establishes the constitutional validation system governing PBOS.

---

Purpose

Validation determines whether constitutional compliance has been objectively demonstrated.

Validation protects constitutional integrity.

Validation shall be independent of implementation.

---

Validation Philosophy

Engineering assertions require evidence.

Engineering evidence requires validation.

Validation requires constitutional authority.

No engineering activity shall advance solely because it completed execution.

Engineering shall advance only after successful constitutional validation.

---

Validation Principles

Validation shall be:

independent;

deterministic;

repeatable;

objective;

evidence based;

traceable;

automated whenever practical;

human reviewable.

Validation shall preserve constitutional integrity before engineering velocity.

---

Validation Domains

PBOS shall validate:

constitutional authority;

founder alignment;

Mission integrity;

artifact lineage;

engineering quality;

security posture;

dependency integrity;

operational readiness;

certification readiness.

---

Validation Outcomes

Every validation shall produce one of the following outcomes.

Certified.

Certified with Observations.

Withheld.

Rejected.

Every outcome shall preserve complete engineering evidence.

---

PBOS Responsibilities

PBOS shall execute constitutional validation continuously throughout every engineering lifecycle.

Validation history shall remain permanent.

Validation criteria shall remain transparent.

---

Definition of Done

Constitutional Validation is complete when every engineering decision is supported by independently verifiable constitutional evidence.
