Authority

This document derives its authority from PBS-CON-000.

It establishes the operational governance model through which constitutional authority is exercised throughout PBOS.

No governance process may conflict with the PBOS Constitution.

---

Purpose

The purpose of this document is to define how constitutional authority is exercised, delegated, enforced, validated, and continuously preserved throughout the lifecycle of every mission, artifact, engineering activity, implementation, and release governed by PBOS.

---

Governance Philosophy

Governance exists to preserve engineering integrity.

Governance is not bureaucracy.

Governance exists to ensure that every engineering decision remains aligned with founder intent, constitutional authority, architectural integrity, security, quality, and long-term maintainability.

Governance shall reduce ambiguity rather than introduce it.

---

Constitutional Governance Model

Governance within PBOS shall operate through successive layers of authority.

Constitution.

Vision.

Charter.

Specifications.

Architecture.

Engineering.

Implementation.

Validation.

Certification.

Release.

Authority flows downward.

Validation flows upward.

No lower layer may contradict a higher layer.

---

Operational Governance

Every engineering activity shall possess:

an identified owner;

documented authority;

defined inputs;

expected outputs;

validation criteria;

certification requirements; and

traceable lineage.

Engineering activities lacking constitutional governance shall not execute.

---

Decision Authority

PBOS governs engineering execution.

Founders govern mission, purpose, values, and strategic direction.

Artificial intelligence assists engineering but possesses no constitutional authority.

Final authority always remains with authorized human decision makers.

---

Governance Responsibilities

PBOS shall:

govern engineering execution;

maintain constitutional integrity;

preserve artifact lineage;

validate engineering decisions;

prevent unauthorized execution;

record governance evidence; and

continuously improve governance through constitutional evolution.

---

Validation Rules

Governance validation shall verify:

constitutional alignment;

artifact authority;

engineering lineage;

dependency integrity;

decision traceability;

ownership;

and certification readiness.

Validation failures shall prevent certification.

---

Definition of Done

Governance is considered operational when every engineering activity executed by PBOS possesses identifiable constitutional authority, deterministic governance, and complete traceability.

---

Future Evolution

Governance may evolve through constitutional amendment while preserving constitutional authority and engineering determinism.
