Constitutional Knowledge Graph

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Declaration

The Constitutional Knowledge Graph is the canonical semantic representation of
everything PBOS Genesis knows about an organization.

The Organization Genome defines what an organization is.

The Knowledge Graph defines what PBOS understands about that organization.

Every constitutional subsystem shall consume the Knowledge Graph.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Purpose

The Constitutional Knowledge Graph transforms constitutional organizational
information into a governed semantic graph capable of supporting reasoning,
planning, engineering, validation, certification, release, and autonomous
organizational evolution.

The Knowledge Graph becomes the constitutional memory of PBOS Genesis.
