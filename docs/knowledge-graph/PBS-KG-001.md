Knowledge Principles

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Declaration

Knowledge Principles establish the immutable constitutional laws governing
organizational knowledge within PBOS Genesis.

Every knowledge relationship shall satisfy these principles.

Canonical Principles

Constitution Before Knowledge.

Identity Before Relationships.

Meaning Before Storage.

Evidence Before Inference.

Governance Before Intelligence.

Version Before Mutation.

Determinism Before Performance.

Human Authority Before Autonomous Learning.
