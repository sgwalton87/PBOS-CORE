Knowledge Authority

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Declaration

Knowledge Authority establishes the constitutional authority governing what
PBOS Genesis may know, infer, preserve, publish, and utilize throughout the
organizational lifecycle.

No organizational knowledge shall become constitutional knowledge without
Knowledge Authority.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Purpose

Knowledge Authority defines who may establish constitutional knowledge, how
knowledge becomes authoritative, how inference is approved, how semantic
relationships are protected, and how constitutional understanding evolves.

PBOS Responsibilities

PBOS shall:

establish Knowledge Authority;

Definition of Done

Knowledge Authority is complete when every constitutional knowledge artifact
is governed through constitutional authority with complete evidence,
traceability, explainability, and organizational integrity.
