Knowledge Services

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Declaration

Knowledge Services provide the constitutional capabilities responsible for
constructing, maintaining, querying, reasoning over, and governing the
Knowledge Graph.

Every service shall execute as an independently governed constitutional
service.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Purpose

Knowledge Services make constitutional organizational knowledge continuously
available to every subsystem within PBOS Genesis.

The Knowledge Graph shall become a shared constitutional utility.
