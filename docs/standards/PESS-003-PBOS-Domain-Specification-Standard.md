# PBOS Domain Specification Standard (PESS-003)

> Constitutional Domains define enduring organizational capability.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

## Purpose

Domains partition PBOS Genesis into governed constitutional capability areas.

Domains SHALL own constitutional responsibility.

Domains SHALL NOT overlap constitutional authority.

## Domain Responsibilities

Define constitutional authority.

Define capability boundaries.

Govern subordinate engines.

Govern subordinate compilers.

Govern certification.

## Constitutional Law

Every Constitutional Engine SHALL belong to exactly one Domain.

Authority SHALL flow from Domain to Engine.
