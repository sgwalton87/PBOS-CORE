# PBOS Artifact Specification Standard (PESS-004)

> Every artifact produced by PBOS Genesis SHALL be governed.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

## Purpose

Artifacts are immutable constitutional outputs produced by Constitutional
Engines and Constitutional Compilers.

Artifacts SHALL remain:

• traceable

• explainable

• certifiable

• reproducible

• versioned

## Constitutional Law

Artifacts SHALL remain immutable after certification.

Every artifact SHALL preserve complete constitutional lineage.

Artifacts SHALL be independently explainable.
