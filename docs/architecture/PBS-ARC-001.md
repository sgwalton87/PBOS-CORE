Architectural Principles

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Declaration

Architecture governs structure.

Structure governs engineering.

Engineering governs implementation.

PBOS Genesis derives architecture from constitutional organizational intent rather than technological preference.

Architecture shall remain the enduring structural truth of every organization engineered by PBOS.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Purpose

Architectural principles establish permanent engineering laws.

These principles ensure that every organization engineered by PBOS possesses coherent structure, deterministic evolution, constitutional integrity, and explainable behavior.

Architectural principles shall outlive implementation technologies.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Canonical Principles

Organization Before Technology.

Mission Before Capability.

Capability Before Component.

Relationship Before Implementation.

Architecture Before Engineering.

Engineering Before Code.

Code Before Deployment.

Validation Before Certification.

Certification Before Release.

Evolution Before Replacement.

Knowledge Before Automation.

Governance Before Optimization.

Constitution Before Everything.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Architectural Responsibilities

Architecture shall:

organize complexity;

eliminate ambiguity;

preserve organizational intent;

maximize composability;

enable autonomous engineering;

support deterministic compilation;

enable constitutional reasoning;

support future technological evolution.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Architectural Quality

Every architecture shall be:

Deterministic.

Composable.

Observable.

Secure.

Governed.

Versioned.

Traceable.

Explainable.

Resilient.

Extensible.

Technology Independent.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Definition of Done

Architectural Principles are complete when every engineering system generated by PBOS demonstrates constitutional consistency, structural coherence, deterministic behavior, and long-term maintainability.
