Architecture Graph

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Declaration

The Architecture Graph is the canonical structural representation of every organization engineered by PBOS Genesis.

Architecture is not documentation.

Architecture is a governed knowledge graph.

PBOS shall reason over graph structures rather than isolated engineering artifacts.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Purpose

The Architecture Graph transforms constitutional organizational knowledge into deterministic engineering structure.

The graph represents organizational intelligence independently of implementation technology.

Repositories, source code, infrastructure, and documentation become projections of the Architecture Graph.
