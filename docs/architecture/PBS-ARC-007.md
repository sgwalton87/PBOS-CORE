Architectural Evolution

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Declaration

Architecture is a living constitutional system.

Architectures shall evolve continuously while preserving constitutional integrity, organizational intent, engineering lineage, and operational continuity.

PBOS Genesis shall evolve architecture without sacrificing determinism.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Purpose

Architectural evolution ensures organizations remain adaptable without abandoning institutional knowledge.

PBOS Genesis shall preserve architectural continuity across every generation of technology.

Evolution shall strengthen architecture rather than replace it.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Evolution Principles

Every architectural evolution shall:

preserve constitutional authority;

preserve organizational intent;

preserve engineering lineage;

minimize structural disruption;

maintain backward compatibility whenever practical;

improve engineering quality;

increase organizational understanding.

PBOS Responsibilities

PBOS shall:

analyze architectural impact;

predict engineering consequences;

coordinate evolution planning;

protect organizational continuity;

record architectural history;

preserve institutional knowledge indefinitely.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Validation

Architectural evolution shall fail if constitutional authority, engineering lineage, or organizational integrity cannot be preserved.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Definition of Done

Architectural Evolution is complete when organizations continuously improve their structural intelligence without compromising constitutional authority or engineering continuity.
