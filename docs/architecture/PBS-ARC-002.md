Architectural Layers

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Declaration

Every organization engineered by PBOS shall possess a deterministic architectural hierarchy.

Layers establish responsibility.

Responsibilities establish engineering boundaries.

Engineering boundaries eliminate ambiguity.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Purpose

Architectural layers separate organizational concerns while preserving complete constitutional traceability.

Every layer possesses one primary responsibility.

Responsibilities shall not overlap.

Layer Independence

Each layer shall:

possess explicit responsibilities;

expose governed interfaces;

remain independently evolvable;

preserve constitutional authority;

remain observable;

remain traceable.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Definition of Done

Architectural Layers are complete when every engineering responsibility exists within exactly one constitutional layer while preserving deterministic organizational engineering.
