Architectural Domains

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Declaration

Organizations consist of domains.

Domains encapsulate organizational knowledge.

PBOS Genesis shall engineer domains rather than isolated software components.

Domains represent enduring business capabilities.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Purpose

Architectural domains establish the organizational boundaries through which engineering systems evolve independently while preserving constitutional coherence.

Domains organize complexity.

Domains preserve ownership.

Domains support autonomous engineering.

Domain Responsibilities

Every domain shall possess:

constitutional authority;

defined ownership;

engineering objectives;

public interfaces;

dependency declarations;

validation requirements;

certification requirements;

evolution strategy.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Engineering Responsibilities

PBOS shall:

discover domains;

construct domain graphs;

coordinate domain evolution;

prevent domain coupling;

maintain domain integrity;

support autonomous domain engineering.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Definition of Done

Architectural Domains are complete when every organizational capability belongs to a governed architectural domain possessing explicit ownership, deterministic boundaries, and constitutional authority.
