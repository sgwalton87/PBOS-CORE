Architectural Patterns

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Declaration

Architectural Patterns are reusable constitutional engineering solutions.

Patterns preserve organizational intelligence.

Patterns eliminate unnecessary architectural reinvention.

Patterns accelerate governed engineering.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Purpose

PBOS Genesis shall recognize recurring organizational structures and represent them as governed architectural patterns.

Patterns become reusable engineering knowledge.

Engineering shall reuse proven organizational intelligence whenever practical.

Pattern Requirements

Every pattern shall possess:

constitutional authority;

organizational purpose;

engineering rationale;

defined applicability;

known limitations;

validation criteria;

evolution strategy;

engineering evidence.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

PBOS Responsibilities

PBOS shall:

identify reusable patterns;

recommend architectural patterns;

compose patterns;

validate pattern compatibility;

record pattern evolution;

continuously improve organizational engineering.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Validation

Patterns shall remain independently certifiable.

Pattern misuse shall fail architectural validation.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Definition of Done

Architectural Patterns are complete when reusable organizational intelligence becomes a governed engineering asset continuously available to PBOS Genesis.
