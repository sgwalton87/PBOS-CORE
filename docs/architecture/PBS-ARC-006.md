Architectural Decisions

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Declaration

Every architectural decision shall possess constitutional authority, documented rationale, measurable consequences, and permanent engineering lineage.

Architecture shall never depend upon undocumented assumptions.

PBOS Genesis shall preserve architectural knowledge indefinitely.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Purpose

Architectural Decisions transform organizational intent into durable engineering guidance.

Every decision shall be explainable.

Every decision shall remain reviewable.

Every decision shall support future organizational evolution.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Decision Requirements

Every architectural decision shall define:

problem statement;

organizational objective;

constitutional authority;

considered alternatives;

selected approach;

engineering rationale;

expected consequences;

trade-offs;

validation strategy;

future evolution guidance.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Decision Principles

Transparent.

Evidence Based.

Deterministic.

Reviewable.

Traceable.

Versioned.

Governed.

Repeatable.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

PBOS Responsibilities

PBOS shall:

record decisions;

construct decision lineage;

associate decisions with Missions;

associate decisions with Specifications;

associate decisions with Components;

support future reasoning.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Validation

Architectural decisions lacking rationale shall fail constitutional validation.

Engineering shall never implement undocumented architectural decisions.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Definition of Done

Architectural Decisions are complete when every engineering implementation can be traced to an explicitly governed architectural decision supported by constitutional authority and measurable engineering evidence.
