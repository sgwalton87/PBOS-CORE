Architectural Relationships

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Declaration

Organizations are networks of governed relationships.

Relationships define organizational intelligence.

Components possess meaning because of their relationships.

PBOS Genesis shall engineer relationships before implementations.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Purpose

Architectural Relationships establish the structural connections between constitutional artifacts, organizational capabilities, engineering systems, runtime services, and future autonomous components.

PBOS shall reason over relationships rather than isolated artifacts.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Relationship Philosophy

Relationships define context.

Context defines meaning.

Meaning defines engineering.

Engineering produces implementation.

Removing relationships destroys organizational understanding.

Relationship Requirements

Every relationship shall declare:

origin;

destination;

relationship type;

constitutional authority;

engineering purpose;

validation status;

version compatibility.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

PBOS Responsibilities

PBOS shall:

construct relationship graphs;

detect broken relationships;

prevent circular ambiguity;

calculate engineering impact;

support autonomous graph reasoning.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Validation

Undefined relationships shall fail validation.

Ambiguous relationships shall fail certification.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Definition of Done

Architectural Relationships are complete when every engineering artifact participates in an explicit, deterministic, constitutionally governed relationship graph.
