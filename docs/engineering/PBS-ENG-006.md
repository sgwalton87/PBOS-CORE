Engineering Evidence System

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Declaration

Engineering trust shall be established through evidence.

Assertions are insufficient.

Implementation is insufficient.

Evidence establishes confidence.

Evidence authorizes validation.

Evidence enables certification.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Purpose

The Engineering Evidence System continuously captures objective engineering knowledge throughout the complete organizational engineering lifecycle.

Evidence preserves organizational memory.

Evidence enables autonomous reasoning.

Evidence supports constitutional governance.

Evidence Characteristics

Evidence shall be:

Immutable.

Verifiable.

Traceable.

Versioned.

Timestamped.

Governed.

Machine Readable.

Human Readable.

Continuously Accessible.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

PBOS Responsibilities

PBOS shall:

collect evidence;

index evidence;

verify evidence;

associate evidence with constitutional authority;

associate evidence with Missions;

construct evidence graphs;

support engineering audits;

preserve evidence indefinitely.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Validation

Engineering lacking objective evidence shall fail validation.

Certification shall never rely upon undocumented engineering activity.

Evidence shall remain continuously verifiable.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Definition of Done

The Engineering Evidence System is complete when every engineering activity performed by PBOS Genesis produces objective, verifiable, constitutionally governed engineering evidence.
