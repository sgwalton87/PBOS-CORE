Engineering Principles

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Declaration

Engineering Principles establish the permanent laws governing every engineering activity performed by PBOS Genesis.

These principles remain independent of programming language, framework, repository structure, implementation engine, or future technology.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Canonical Principles

Constitution Before Engineering.

Organization Before Software.

Mission Before Task.

Specification Before Architecture.

Architecture Before Engineering.

Engineering Before Implementation.

Validation Before Certification.

Certification Before Release.

Knowledge Before Automation.

Governance Before Optimization.

Determinism Before Velocity.

Human Authority Before Artificial Intelligence.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Engineering Standards

Engineering shall be:

deterministic;

repeatable;

traceable;

observable;

measurable;

governed;

explainable;

composable;

versioned;

continuously evolvable.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

PBOS Responsibilities

PBOS shall continuously evaluate engineering against these principles.

Engineering violating these principles shall fail constitutional validation.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Definition of Done

Engineering Principles are complete when every engineering activity continuously satisfies these constitutional laws.
