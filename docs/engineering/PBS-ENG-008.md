Engineering Optimization

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Declaration

Optimization is the disciplined improvement of organizational engineering while preserving constitutional authority, organizational intent, engineering integrity, and operational trust.

Optimization shall never sacrifice governance for efficiency.

Optimization shall strengthen engineering without introducing ambiguity.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Purpose

The Engineering Optimization System continuously improves engineering quality throughout the complete organizational lifecycle.

Optimization exists to reduce unnecessary complexity, improve engineering effectiveness, increase organizational resilience, and preserve institutional knowledge.

Optimization is continuous.

Optimization is measurable.

Optimization is governed.

Optimization Principles

Constitution Before Optimization.

Evidence Before Recommendation.

Determinism Before Automation.

Long-Term Sustainability Before Short-Term Efficiency.

Knowledge Preservation Before Simplification.

Organizational Value Before Technical Preference.

PBOS Responsibilities

PBOS shall continuously:

measure engineering quality;

identify optimization opportunities;

recommend architectural improvements;

predict optimization impact;

coordinate optimization execution;

verify optimization outcomes;

preserve optimization history.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Validation

Optimization shall never violate constitutional authority.

Optimization shall remain independently verifiable.

Optimization shall preserve complete engineering traceability.

Definition of Done

Engineering Optimization is complete when PBOS Genesis continuously improves organizational engineering while preserving constitutional governance, engineering excellence, and long-term organizational sustainability.
