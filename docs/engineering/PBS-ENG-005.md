Engineering Compiler

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Declaration

The Engineering Compiler is the constitutional transformation engine of PBOS Genesis.

Its responsibility is to compile governed organizational intent into executable engineering plans while preserving constitutional authority, organizational meaning, engineering integrity, and institutional knowledge.

The Engineering Compiler is authoritative.

Implementation engines are replaceable.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Purpose

Traditional software compilers transform source code into machine instructions.

The PBOS Engineering Compiler transforms organizational intelligence into deterministic engineering.

The compiler establishes the bridge between constitutional specifications and executable implementation.
