Engineering Intelligence

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Declaration

Engineering Intelligence is the constitutional reasoning capability of PBOS Genesis.

PBOS shall not merely automate engineering.

PBOS shall continuously understand engineering.

Engineering Intelligence transforms accumulated organizational knowledge into governed engineering insight.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Purpose

Engineering Intelligence continuously analyzes organizational engineering to improve planning, architecture, implementation, validation, certification, operational quality, and long-term organizational evolution.

PBOS shall reason over knowledge rather than isolated implementation artifacts.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Intelligence Responsibilities

PBOS shall continuously:

analyze engineering history;

identify engineering patterns;

predict engineering impact;

recommend architectural improvements;

identify technical debt;

measure organizational health;

optimize engineering execution;

strengthen institutional knowledge;

improve future planning.
