Engineering Planner

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Declaration

The Engineering Planner is the constitutional orchestration engine of PBOS Genesis.

It transforms organizational intent into executable engineering plans.

Planning is a governed engineering activity.

Planning shall precede execution.

Planning shall remain deterministic.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Purpose

The Engineering Planner coordinates organizational engineering through constitutional reasoning rather than manual task management.

Planning shall derive from:

Organization Genome;

Mission Graph;

Specifications;

Architecture Graph;

Engineering Execution Graph.

Planning shall never originate from implementation artifacts.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Planning Responsibilities

PBOS shall:

identify executable Missions;

calculate engineering dependencies;

estimate engineering impact;

prioritize organizational value;

schedule engineering execution;

coordinate implementation engines;

maintain engineering continuity.
