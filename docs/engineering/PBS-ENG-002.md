Engineering Lifecycle

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Declaration

Engineering is a governed lifecycle rather than a sequence of disconnected implementation activities.

PBOS Genesis shall coordinate every engineering activity through a deterministic constitutional lifecycle.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Lifecycle Requirements

Each stage shall define:

inputs;

outputs;

dependencies;

governance;

validation;

evidence;

success criteria;

handoff requirements.

No stage may be skipped.

No stage may self-certify.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

PBOS Responsibilities

PBOS shall:

coordinate lifecycle progression;

prevent invalid transitions;

collect engineering evidence;

maintain lifecycle history;

calculate engineering readiness;

support autonomous execution.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Definition of Done

The Engineering Lifecycle is complete when every engineering activity progresses deterministically from organizational intent through operational excellence without loss of constitutional authority or engineering traceability.
