Engineering Execution Graph

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Declaration

The Engineering Execution Graph is the canonical executable representation of organizational engineering within PBOS Genesis.

The Execution Graph transforms architectural knowledge into executable engineering behavior.

PBOS shall execute graphs rather than isolated engineering tasks.

Engineering behavior shall be deterministic.

Engineering state shall be observable.

Engineering execution shall preserve constitutional authority.

Purpose

The Engineering Execution Graph coordinates every engineering activity performed by PBOS Genesis.

Execution shall never depend upon execution order alone.

Execution shall depend upon validated engineering relationships.

The graph establishes engineering behavior before implementation begins.
