Constitutional Engineering

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Declaration

Constitutional Engineering is the governing engineering discipline of PBOS Genesis.

Engineering shall derive from constitutional authority.

Engineering shall derive from organizational intent.

Engineering shall derive from architecture.

Engineering shall never originate from implementation.

PBOS Genesis transforms governed intent into deterministic engineering.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Purpose

Constitutional Engineering exists to eliminate ambiguity throughout the engineering lifecycle.

Organizations define intent.

PBOS derives engineering.

Implementation engines produce executable systems.

Engineering remains the constitutional bridge between organizational intelligence and software.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Engineering Philosophy

Engineering exists to preserve intent.

Engineering exists to preserve structure.

Engineering exists to preserve knowledge.

Engineering exists to preserve trust.

Implementation technologies may evolve.

Engineering principles shall endure.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Engineering Responsibilities

Engineering shall:

derive from specifications;

respect architecture;

produce deterministic outcomes;

maintain constitutional authority;

preserve institutional knowledge;

support autonomous execution;

generate measurable engineering evidence.
