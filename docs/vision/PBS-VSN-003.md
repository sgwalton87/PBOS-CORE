Authority

This artifact derives its authority from the PBOS Constitution and the PBOS Vision.

It establishes the permanent engineering philosophy governing every capability developed within PBOS.

---

Purpose

Engineering within PBOS exists to transform constitutional intent into production-quality software through deterministic, explainable, and governed execution.

Engineering is a disciplined system rather than a collection of implementation tasks.

---

Engineering Philosophy

Engineering begins long before software is written.

Quality is established through architecture rather than inspection.

Documentation precedes implementation.

Architecture precedes engineering.

Engineering precedes coding.

Validation precedes certification.

Certification precedes release.

Long-term stewardship precedes short-term optimization.

---

Engineering Principles

Engineering shall remain:

Constitutional.

Deterministic.

Composable.

Traceable.

Observable.

Governed.

Explainable.

Testable.

Secure.

Maintainable.

Continuously improving.

---

Engineering Responsibility

PBOS shall engineer systems that remain understandable long after their original implementation.

Every engineering decision shall preserve institutional knowledge.

Every implementation shall possess an architectural rationale.

Every architectural decision shall possess constitutional authority.

---

Engineering Excellence

Engineering excellence is measured by:

Correctness.

Reliability.

Maintainability.

Traceability.

Governance.

Security.

Extensibility.

Determinism.

Long-term sustainability.

Velocity shall never supersede engineering integrity.

---

Validation

PBOS shall continuously evaluate engineering decisions against this philosophy.

Engineering activities that violate these principles shall fail validation.

---

Definition of Done

Engineering Philosophy is complete when every engineering activity governed by PBOS demonstrably reflects these principles from planning through certification.
