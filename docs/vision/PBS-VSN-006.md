Authority

This artifact derives its authority from the PBOS Constitution.

It establishes the permanent philosophy governing quality throughout PBOS.

---

Purpose

Quality is not a phase.

Quality is not a department.

Quality is a constitutional property of every engineering artifact produced by PBOS.

Every engineering activity shall contribute to quality.

Every engineering decision shall preserve quality.

---

Quality Philosophy

Quality begins before implementation.

Quality is established through founder intent.

Quality is refined through architecture.

Quality is protected through engineering.

Quality is verified through validation.

Quality is demonstrated through certification.

Quality is delivered through release.

PBOS shall pursue quality continuously rather than retrospectively.

---

Quality Principles

Quality shall be:

Intentional.

Deterministic.

Observable.

Explainable.

Repeatable.

Measurable.

Secure.

Accessible.

Maintainable.

Extensible.

Reliable.

Recoverable.

Governed.

---

Engineering Quality

Every engineering artifact shall possess:

A documented purpose.

Constitutional authority.

Engineering ownership.

Validation criteria.

Certification criteria.

Complete traceability.

Deterministic behavior.

Long-term maintainability.

---

Continuous Improvement

Quality is never complete.

Every engineering cycle shall improve the engineering operating system.

Every completed Mission shall strengthen future Missions.

Institutional knowledge shall accumulate.

Technical debt shall continuously decrease.

---

Validation

PBOS shall continuously evaluate quality throughout planning, engineering, implementation, validation, certification, release, and operational evolution.

Quality shall never depend solely upon testing.

---

Definition of Done

Quality Philosophy is complete when quality becomes an inherent constitutional property of every engineering artifact governed by PBOS.
