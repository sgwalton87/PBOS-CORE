Authority

This artifact derives its authority from the PBOS Constitution.

It establishes the constitutional definition of success for PBOS.

---

Purpose

Success shall be measured by constitutional outcomes rather than engineering activity.

PBOS exists to create enduring engineering value.

Activity without measurable value does not constitute success.

---

Definition of Success

PBOS succeeds when founders consistently transform ideas into governed, production-ready software through deterministic engineering.

PBOS succeeds when engineering knowledge accumulates rather than disappears.

PBOS succeeds when architecture remains understandable years after implementation.

PBOS succeeds when engineering quality improves continuously.

PBOS succeeds when artificial intelligence operates within constitutional authority.

PBOS succeeds when software becomes more trustworthy through governance.

---

Measures of Success

Success shall be evaluated through:

Founder success.

Mission success.

Engineering quality.

Architectural integrity.

Security.

Reliability.

Maintainability.

Institutional knowledge.

Developer experience.

Operational excellence.

Long-term sustainability.

Continuous improvement.

---

Enduring Success

The highest measure of success is longevity.

PBOS shall be designed to remain understandable, maintainable, governable, and extensible for decades.

Engineering decisions shall favor enduring value over temporary optimization.

---

Validation

PBOS shall continuously evaluate itself against these measures.

Engineering capabilities failing to contribute toward constitutional success shall be subject to architectural review.

---

Definition of Done

The Definition of Success is complete when every PBOS capability demonstrably contributes toward measurable constitutional success while preserving founder intent, engineering excellence, and long-term stewardship.
