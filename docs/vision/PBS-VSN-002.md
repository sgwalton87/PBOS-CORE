Authority

This artifact derives its authority from the PBOS Constitution and the PBOS Vision.

It establishes the constitutional philosophy governing Missions throughout PBOS.

---

Purpose

PBOS engineers Missions.

Products, applications, platforms, repositories, and services are outcomes of successful Missions.

Every engineering activity begins with a Mission.

---

Mission Philosophy

A Mission is the complete expression of founder intent.

A Mission defines what shall exist, why it shall exist, who it serves, how success is measured, and the constitutional authority under which it shall be engineered.

Missions exist independently of implementation.

Repositories may change.

Technologies may evolve.

Programming languages may be replaced.

The Mission endures.

---

Mission Principles

Every Mission shall possess:

A defined purpose.

A measurable outcome.

A governing founder.

A constitutional authority.

A deterministic engineering plan.

A complete engineering lineage.

Independent versioning.

Independent certification.

Independent evolution.

---

Mission Lifecycle

Every Mission progresses through:

Vision.

Definition.

Specification.

Architecture.

Planning.

Engineering.

Validation.

Certification.

Release.

Evolution.

PBOS governs each phase.

---

Mission Independence

No Mission shall depend upon a specific programming language, framework, vendor, cloud provider, or repository.

Engineering decisions shall serve the Mission rather than constrain it.

---

Validation

PBOS shall continuously validate that every engineering artifact belongs to an identifiable Mission.

Artifacts lacking Mission lineage shall fail constitutional validation.

---

Definition of Done

Mission Philosophy is complete when every engineering artifact can trace its existence to an identifiable Mission governed by constitutional authority.
