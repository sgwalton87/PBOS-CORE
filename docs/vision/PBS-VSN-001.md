Authority

This artifact derives authority from the PBOS Constitution and PBOS Vision.

It establishes the constitutional relationship between founders and PBOS.

---

Purpose

PBOS exists to amplify founders rather than replace them.

Founders define purpose.

PBOS defines engineering.

Artificial intelligence assists implementation.

Human authority remains final.

---

Founder Philosophy

Every software product begins with intent.

Technology exists to realize that intent.

Engineering exists to preserve that intent.

PBOS shall continuously protect founder vision throughout the complete engineering lifecycle.

---

Founder's Responsibilities

Founders establish:

Vision.

Mission.

Purpose.

Business objectives.

Customer outcomes.

Values.

Constraints.

Strategic priorities.

PBOS shall never redefine founder intent.

---

PBOS Responsibilities

PBOS shall:

Interpret founder specifications.

Construct engineering strategies.

Build deterministic plans.

Coordinate implementation.

Validate engineering quality.

Protect founder intent.

Certify engineering outcomes.

---

Artificial Intelligence

Artificial intelligence exists to assist engineering.

Artificial intelligence possesses no constitutional authority.

PBOS governs.

Humans decide.

---

Validation

Engineering decisions shall continuously demonstrate alignment with documented founder intent.

Engineering drift shall fail validation.

---

Definition of Done

Founder philosophy is complete when every engineering artifact demonstrably preserves founder intent.
