Authority

This artifact derives its authority from the PBOS Constitution.

It establishes the long-term future direction of PBOS beyond current technologies, implementation strategies, and engineering practices.

---

Purpose

PBOS is designed to outlive today's software tools, programming languages, cloud providers, artificial intelligence models, and computing platforms.

Its constitutional principles shall remain stable while its engineering capabilities continuously evolve.

---

Future Vision

PBOS shall become the universal engineering operating system capable of governing the complete lifecycle of software creation for individuals, startups, enterprises, governments, educational institutions, nonprofit organizations, and future organizations not yet imagined.

PBOS shall engineer products rather than code.

PBOS shall preserve institutional knowledge across generations of technology.

PBOS shall continuously improve without abandoning constitutional authority.

---

Technology Independence

PBOS shall remain independent of:

Programming languages.

Frameworks.

Cloud providers.

Database technologies.

Artificial intelligence providers.

Infrastructure vendors.

Development environments.

PBOS shall adopt technology without becoming dependent upon it.

---

Global Vision

PBOS shall establish a universal constitutional engineering framework capable of supporting engineering teams throughout the world.

Engineering principles shall remain consistent regardless of language, geography, organization, or technology stack.

---

Evolution

PBOS shall continuously evolve through governed constitutional improvement.

Evolution shall strengthen constitutional integrity.

Evolution shall preserve backward compatibility whenever practical.

Evolution shall improve engineering quality.

---

Validation

Every strategic evolution shall demonstrate measurable advancement toward this Future Vision.

Regression from constitutional principles shall fail validation.

---

Definition of Done

Future Vision is complete when PBOS remains capable of engineering future technologies without requiring constitutional redesign.
