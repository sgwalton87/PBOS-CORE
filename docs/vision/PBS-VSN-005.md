Authority

This artifact derives its authority from the PBOS Constitution and establishes the philosophy governing every product engineered through PBOS.

---

Purpose

Products engineered by PBOS shall exist to solve meaningful human problems through exceptional engineering, thoughtful design, and measurable outcomes.

Technology is a means.

Human value is the objective.

---

Product Philosophy

Every product begins with a problem worth solving.

Every feature shall possess a documented purpose.

Every capability shall contribute measurable value.

Every implementation shall trace to a validated specification.

Every release shall improve the experience of its intended users.

---

Product Principles

Products engineered by PBOS shall be:

Purpose Driven.

Mission Aligned.

Human Centered.

Secure by Design.

Accessible.

Reliable.

Scalable.

Composable.

Maintainable.

Continuously Evolving.

---

Product Quality

Quality shall not be measured solely by functionality.

Quality includes:

Clarity.

Usability.

Reliability.

Performance.

Accessibility.

Security.

Maintainability.

Extensibility.

Trustworthiness.

Long-term sustainability.

---

Engineering Relationship

Architecture exists to enable products.

Engineering exists to realize architecture.

Implementation exists to realize engineering.

Every layer serves the Mission.

---

Validation

PBOS shall continuously verify that every engineered capability contributes directly to documented product objectives.

Capabilities lacking measurable value shall be subject to architectural review.

---

Definition of Done

Product Philosophy is complete when every product engineered through PBOS demonstrably advances founder intent, serves its users, and satisfies constitutional engineering standards.
