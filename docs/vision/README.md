Overview

The Vision Volume establishes the long-term direction of PBOS.

Where the Constitution defines authority, the Vision defines aspiration.

Every architectural decision, engineering activity, implementation strategy, and autonomous capability shall contribute toward realizing the Vision established within this volume.

The Vision answers a single question.

"What is PBOS becoming?"

Purpose

The Vision exists to provide enduring direction for the evolution of PBOS.

Technology changes.

Engineering practices evolve.

Artificial intelligence advances.

The Vision provides continuity across every future generation of engineering.
