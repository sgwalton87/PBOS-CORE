Validation Principles

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Declaration

Validation Principles establish the immutable laws governing engineering
verification throughout PBOS Genesis.

Every validation decision shall satisfy these principles.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Canonical Principles

Constitution Before Validation.

Evidence Before Approval.

Completeness Before Progress.

Correctness Before Performance.

Determinism Before Automation.

Explainability Before Acceptance.

Continuous Traceability.

Independent Verification.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Validation Guarantees

Every validation shall remain:

Independent.

Repeatable.

Deterministic.

Auditable.

Recoverable.

Versioned.

Observable.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

PBOS Responsibilities

PBOS shall continuously validate engineering correctness before permitting
subsequent constitutional phases.

Definition of Done

Validation Principles are complete when every engineering verification
performed by PBOS Genesis satisfies constitutional engineering standards.
