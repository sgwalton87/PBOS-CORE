Validation Governance

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Declaration

Validation Governance establishes the constitutional authority governing all
verification activities performed by PBOS Genesis.

Validation decisions shall be transparent.

Validation decisions shall be reproducible.

Validation decisions shall be constitutionally governed.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Purpose

Validation Governance defines the policies, authority, accountability,
decision-making responsibilities, and evidence requirements governing
constitutional engineering verification.

Governance Principles

Constitution Before Validation.

Continuous Accountability.

PBOS Responsibilities

PBOS shall:

govern validation execution;

maintain validation policies;

publish validation decisions;

record governance evidence;

support constitutional audits;

preserve engineering trust.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Definition of Done

Validation Governance is complete when every engineering verification is
performed under constitutional authority with complete transparency,
traceability, accountability, and engineering evidence.
