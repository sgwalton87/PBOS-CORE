Adaptive Planning

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Declaration

Adaptive Planning enables PBOS Genesis to continuously refine engineering
planning through governed organizational learning.

Planning shall adapt.

The Constitution shall remain immutable.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Purpose

Adaptive Planning transforms engineering evidence, founder guidance,
organizational evolution, and certified outcomes into progressively stronger
engineering strategies.
