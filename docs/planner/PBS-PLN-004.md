Dependency Resolution

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Declaration

Dependency Resolution governs how PBOS Genesis identifies, validates,
prioritizes, and satisfies engineering dependencies.

Engineering shall never execute out of constitutional order.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Purpose

Dependency Resolution guarantees that every engineering activity executes only
after all constitutional, architectural, technical, organizational, and
operational prerequisites have been satisfied.

Dependencies preserve engineering integrity.

support parallel execution;

prevent invalid execution.

Dependency Resolution is complete when every engineering activity executes
only after all governing dependencies have been satisfied.
