Mission Planning

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Declaration

Mission Planning governs how organizational objectives become executable
engineering missions.

Every engineering activity shall belong to one constitutional mission.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Purpose

Mission Planning converts founder intent, constitutional requirements,
organizational goals, and engineering priorities into governed engineering
missions.
