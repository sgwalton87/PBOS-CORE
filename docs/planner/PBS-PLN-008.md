Planning Optimization

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Declaration

Planning Optimization continuously improves engineering planning while
preserving constitutional authority and deterministic execution.

Optimization shall improve planning.

Optimization shall never alter constitutional truth.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Purpose

Planning Optimization evaluates engineering outcomes, mission completion,
runtime evidence, dependency resolution, and organizational intelligence to
produce increasingly effective engineering plans.
