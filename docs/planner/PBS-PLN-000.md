Constitutional Planner

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Declaration

The Constitutional Planner is the engineering intelligence engine of PBOS Genesis.

The Planner determines what engineering shall occur.

The Runtime determines how engineering executes.

Every engineering activity originates from a governed planning decision.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Purpose

The Planner transforms constitutional intent into deterministic engineering plans.

It continuously evaluates organizational objectives, engineering readiness,
dependencies, and constitutional authority to determine the next valid
engineering action.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Planner Responsibilities

Interpret organizational intent.

Construct mission plans.

Prioritize engineering work.

Resolve dependencies.

Coordinate implementation engines.

Optimize execution order.

Maintain engineering continuity.

Publish planning evidence.
