Work Decomposition

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Declaration

Work Decomposition transforms engineering missions into deterministic,
manageable, certifiable engineering work.

PBOS Genesis shall never execute undefined work.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Purpose

Work Decomposition breaks complex organizational engineering into governed,
dependency-aware engineering activities that can be independently validated,
implemented, certified, and resumed.

PBOS Responsibilities

PBOS shall:

decompose engineering work;

construct dependency graphs;

estimate engineering scope;

identify reusable work;

support parallel engineering;

maintain decomposition lineage.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Definition of Done

Work Decomposition is complete when every engineering mission can be
deterministically decomposed into independently executable engineering work.
