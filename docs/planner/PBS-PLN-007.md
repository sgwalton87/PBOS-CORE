Planning Intelligence

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Declaration

Planning Intelligence is the reasoning capability of PBOS Genesis.

It transforms constitutional knowledge into engineering decisions.

It continuously improves planning quality while preserving constitutional
governance.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Planning Intelligence analyzes engineering evidence, organizational knowledge,
mission progress, dependencies, historical outcomes, and runtime observations
to improve future engineering plans.
