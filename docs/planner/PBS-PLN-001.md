Planning Principles

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Declaration

Planning Principles establish the immutable laws governing engineering planning.

Every planning decision shall satisfy these principles.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Canonical Principles

Constitution Before Planning.

Mission Before Tasks.

Dependencies Before Execution.

Evidence Before Completion.

Determinism Before Speed.

Quality Before Quantity.

Validation Before Progress.

Human Authority Before Autonomous Decisions.

Continuous Traceability.

Continuous Explainability.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Planning Guarantees

Planning shall remain:

Deterministic.

Observable.

Governed.

Recoverable.

Versioned.

Explainable.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

PBOS Responsibilities

PBOS shall continuously validate every engineering plan against these
constitutional principles.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Definition of Done

Planning Principles are complete when every engineering plan produced by PBOS
Genesis satisfies constitutional governance.
