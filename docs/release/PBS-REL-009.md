Release Authority

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Declaration

Release Authority establishes the constitutional authority governing the
publication of engineering throughout PBOS Genesis.

No engineering shall become an official constitutional release without
Release Authority.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Purpose

Release Authority defines who may authorize publication, how release
authority is delegated, how publication decisions are governed, and how
constitutional trust is preserved throughout organizational evolution.

PBOS Responsibilities

PBOS shall:

govern constitutional publication;

authorize official releases;

publish release evidence;

preserve publication lineage;

support constitutional audits;

maintain organizational trust.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Definition of Done

Release Authority is complete when every constitutional release is published
under governed authority with complete engineering evidence,
organizational provenance, traceability, accountability, and trust.
