Constitutional Engineering Language (CEL)

The Programming Language of Organizations

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Declaration

The Constitutional Engineering Language (CEL) is the canonical engineering language of PBOS Genesis.

CEL is not a programming language.

CEL is a constitutional language used to describe organizations, engineering systems, missions, products, architecture, operational behavior, governance, and institutional intent.

PBOS Genesis compiles CEL into production-ready engineering systems.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Purpose

CEL exists to eliminate ambiguity between organizational vision and software implementation.

Organizations shall describe themselves once.

PBOS Genesis shall continuously derive engineering from those descriptions.

Engineering shall become deterministic.

Implementation shall become reproducible.

Institutional knowledge shall become permanent.
