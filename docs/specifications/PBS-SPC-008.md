Certification Specification

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Authority

This specification establishes the constitutional certification framework governing engineering readiness throughout PBOS Genesis.

Certification authorizes organizational trust.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Purpose

Certification confirms that engineering satisfies constitutional authority, organizational intent, engineering quality, validation requirements, security expectations, operational readiness, and release governance.

Certification authorizes deployment.
