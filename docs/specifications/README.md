PBOS Engineering Specifications

"The Constitution governs.
The Vision inspires.
The Charter organizes.
Specifications define."

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Overview

The Specification Volume establishes the constitutional engineering language of PBOS Genesis.

Specifications are not documentation.

Specifications are authoritative engineering declarations from which architecture, engineering, validation, certification, runtime behavior, and implementation are derived.

Specifications represent the permanent engineering intent of an organization.

Implementations are temporary.

Specifications are enduring.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Purpose

The purpose of the Specification Volume is to establish a deterministic, implementation-independent language capable of describing organizations, engineering systems, products, missions, capabilities, workflows, security models, artificial intelligence systems, runtime behavior, and future technologies.

PBOS Genesis shall compile engineering from specifications.

Specifications shall remain the constitutional source of engineering truth.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Engineering Philosophy

Traditional software engineering begins with implementation.

PBOS Genesis begins with specifications.

Organizations describe themselves.

Specifications define organizational intent.

Architecture organizes intent.

Engineering operationalizes architecture.

Implementation realizes engineering.

Validation confirms correctness.

Certification establishes trust.

Release delivers capability.

Operational knowledge continuously improves future specifications.
