Engineering Traceability Specification

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Authority

This specification establishes the canonical traceability model governing every engineering artifact, engineering activity, implementation, validation result, certification outcome, and release produced by PBOS Genesis.

Traceability is a constitutional requirement.

Every engineering decision shall be explainable.

Every engineering artifact shall possess complete lineage.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Purpose

PBOS Genesis shall preserve complete engineering history.

Engineering knowledge shall accumulate.

Institutional knowledge shall never be lost through personnel changes, technological evolution, repository restructuring, or implementation replacement.

Engineering shall always remain explainable.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Traceability Philosophy

Every engineering artifact exists because another artifact required its existence.

Nothing shall exist without lineage.

Nothing shall exist without constitutional authority.

Nothing shall exist without engineering purpose.

PBOS shall continuously preserve these relationships.

Traceability Requirements

Every artifact shall record:

constitutional authority;

originating Mission;

originating specification;

engineering owner;

repository identity;

artifact dependencies;

validation evidence;

certification evidence;

release lineage;

successor artifacts.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Engineering Responsibilities

PBOS shall:

construct lineage graphs;

identify broken lineage;

detect orphaned engineering;

calculate engineering impact;

support repository evolution;

support autonomous reasoning;

preserve institutional knowledge indefinitely.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Validation

Broken lineage shall fail constitutional validation.

Missing engineering history shall prevent certification.

Engineering traceability shall remain continuously verifiable.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Definition of Done

Engineering Traceability is complete when every engineering artifact can be traced deterministically from organizational intent through operational deployment and continuous evolution.
