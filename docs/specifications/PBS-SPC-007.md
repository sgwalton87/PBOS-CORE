Validation Specification

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Authority

This specification establishes the constitutional validation framework governing every engineering activity within PBOS Genesis.

Validation protects engineering integrity.

Validation precedes certification.

Validation is continuous.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Purpose

Validation objectively determines whether engineering satisfies constitutional authority, specifications, architecture, engineering standards, organizational intent, and operational readiness.

Engineering completion does not imply engineering correctness.

Validation establishes confidence.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Validation Philosophy

Engineering assertions require evidence.

Evidence requires validation.

Validation requires constitutional authority.

No engineering artifact shall progress solely because implementation completed.
