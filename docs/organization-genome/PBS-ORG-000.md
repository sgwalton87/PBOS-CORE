Constitutional Organization Genome

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Declaration

The Constitutional Organization Genome is the canonical engineering model of
an organization within PBOS Genesis.

Every organization engineered by PBOS Genesis shall possess exactly one
Organization Genome.

The Organization Genome is the authoritative representation of organizational
identity.

All engineering originates from the Organization Genome.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Purpose

The Organization Genome transforms founder vision into a deterministic,
machine-readable organizational blueprint.

It defines the complete organizational identity consumed by the Planner,
Compiler, Runtime, Validation, Certification, and Release systems.

The Organization Genome becomes the constitutional source of organizational
truth.
