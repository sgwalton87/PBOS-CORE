Genome Authority

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Declaration

Genome Authority establishes the constitutional authority governing the
creation, stewardship, modification, inheritance, protection, and evolution
of every Organization Genome managed by PBOS Genesis.

The Organization Genome is the constitutional DNA of an organization.

No component, implementation engine, artificial intelligence system,
automation workflow, runtime service, engineer, or autonomous process may
modify an Organization Genome outside constitutional authority.

Genome Authority protects organizational identity.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Purpose

Genome Authority defines who possesses authority over an Organization Genome,
how authority is delegated, how constitutional changes are approved, how
organizational lineage is preserved, and how founder intent remains protected
throughout the engineering lifecycle.

Authority preserves trust.

Authority preserves identity.

Authority preserves continuity.

Definition of Done

Genome Authority is complete when every Organization Genome is protected by
constitutional authority such that organizational identity, founder intent,
engineering integrity, governance, and organizational continuity remain
preserved across every generation of the organization.
