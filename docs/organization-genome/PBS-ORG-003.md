Genome Identity

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Declaration

Genome Identity establishes the immutable constitutional identity of every
organization engineered by PBOS Genesis.

Identity exists independently of implementation.

Identity survives technological evolution.

Identity survives organizational growth.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Purpose

Genome Identity defines the permanent characteristics that uniquely identify
an organization throughout its constitutional lifecycle.

Identity becomes the root of all engineering.
