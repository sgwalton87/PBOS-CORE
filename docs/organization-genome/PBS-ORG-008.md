Genome Governance

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Declaration

Genome Governance establishes the constitutional governance model governing
every Organization Genome managed by PBOS Genesis.

The Organization Genome shall remain governed throughout its lifecycle.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Purpose

Genome Governance defines constitutional authority, decision rights,
accountability, evidence requirements, policy enforcement, and organizational
oversight for Genome evolution.

Governance Principles

Constitution Before Governance.

Identity Before Change.

Evidence Before Approval.

Transparency Before Adoption.

Continuous Accountability.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

PBOS Responsibilities

PBOS shall:

govern Genome evolution;

preserve Genome integrity;

publish Genome evidence;

support constitutional audits;

maintain organizational continuity.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Definition of Done

Genome Governance is complete when every Organization Genome evolves under
constitutional governance with complete traceability and accountability.
