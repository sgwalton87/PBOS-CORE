Genome Evolution

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Declaration

Organizations evolve.

Their constitutional identity shall not.

Genome Evolution governs how organizations continuously mature while
preserving constitutional identity, founder intent, organizational lineage,
and engineering integrity.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Purpose

Genome Evolution provides the constitutional framework governing how an
Organization Genome expands, refines, restructures, and matures throughout
its lifecycle.

Evolution shall strengthen the organization.

Evolution shall never replace constitutional truth.
