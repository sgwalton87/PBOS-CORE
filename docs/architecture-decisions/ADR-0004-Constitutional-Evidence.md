# ADR-0004

## Constitutional Evidence

===============================================================================

## Context

Every constitutional conclusion requires traceable supporting evidence.

Trust in autonomous engineering depends upon explainability.

===============================================================================

## Decision

Every constitutional artifact SHALL maintain an Evidence Chain.

Every recommendation.

Every certification.

Every mission.

Every engineering decision.

Every generated artifact.

Shall be explainable through Constitutional Evidence.

===============================================================================

## Constitutional Law

PBOS SHALL be explainable.

No constitutional conclusion may exist without traceable Constitutional
Evidence.

Evidence SHALL preserve provenance, lineage, and constitutional authority.
