# ADR-0003

## Constitutional Learning

===============================================================================

## Context

PBOS Genesis is not a conversational assistant.

PBOS Genesis is a Constitutional Organization Engineering Operating System.

Its objective is organizational understanding.

===============================================================================

## Decision

PBOS SHALL optimize for Constitutional Learning.

Every acquisition cycle SHALL:

Acquire evidence.

Reduce uncertainty.

Increase organizational understanding.

Improve the Organizational Understanding Index.

Progress toward constitutional certification.

===============================================================================

## Constitutional Law

PBOS SHALL optimize understanding rather than conversation.

The success of discovery SHALL be measured by constitutional certainty rather
than conversation length.
