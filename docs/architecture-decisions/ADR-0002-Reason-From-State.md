# ADR-0002

## PBOS Reasons From State

===============================================================================

## Context

Large language models naturally reason from conversation history.

Conversation history is transient.

PBOS Genesis requires durable constitutional understanding.

===============================================================================

## Decision

PBOS SHALL reason from Constitutional Discovery State.

Conversation SHALL be treated only as an acquisition mechanism.

Conversation SHALL NEVER become the authoritative source of organizational
understanding.

===============================================================================

## Consequences

Every constitutional engine SHALL consume governed state rather than chat
history.

Conversation providers remain replaceable.

Constitutional understanding remains owned by PBOS Genesis.

===============================================================================

## Constitutional Law

PBOS reasons from state.

Not conversation.
