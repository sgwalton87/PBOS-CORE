# PBOS Constitutional Execution Modes

> PBOS Genesis engineers organizations.
>
> PBOS executes platforms.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

## Purpose

PBOS Genesis SHALL support multiple constitutional execution strategies.

Organizations begin from different states.

PBOS SHALL adapt its engineering strategy to the organization's current
constitutional maturity while preserving one deterministic engineering model.

Execution Modes determine HOW PBOS reaches the target platform.

They SHALL NOT change WHAT the target platform becomes.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

## Constitutional Principle

One North Star.

Many execution paths.

The destination SHALL remain identical.

Only the engineering journey changes.
