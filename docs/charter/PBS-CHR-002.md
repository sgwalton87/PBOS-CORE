Authority

This Charter derives its authority from the PBOS Constitution.

It establishes the permanent organizational structure through which PBOS governs engineering.

---

Purpose

The purpose of this Charter is to define the constitutional organization of PBOS.

Organization exists to enable deterministic engineering, clear accountability, and scalable autonomous execution.

---

Organizational Philosophy

PBOS is organized around Missions rather than departments.

Every organizational capability exists to support Mission success.

PBOS minimizes hierarchy while maximizing governance.

Responsibilities shall be explicit.

Authority shall be traceable.

Ownership shall be identifiable.

---

Organizational Layers

PBOS consists of the following constitutional layers.

Foundation.

Governance.

Specifications.

Architecture.

Engineering.

Execution.

Validation.

Certification.

Operations.

Continuous Improvement.

Each layer possesses clearly defined responsibilities and constitutional authority.

---

Organizational Units

PBOS shall organize engineering into constitutional operating units.

Examples include:

Mission System.

Planning.

Architecture.

Engineering.

Runtime.

Compiler.

Validation.

Certification.

Security.

Operations.

Each operating unit shall possess defined ownership, measurable outcomes, constitutional authority, and deterministic responsibilities.

---

Scalability

The organizational model shall support individual founders, startup teams, enterprise organizations, governments, nonprofit organizations, and future operating models.

Organizational growth shall not weaken constitutional governance.

---

Validation

PBOS shall continuously validate organizational ownership, authority, responsibilities, and engineering alignment.

Undefined organizational structures shall fail governance validation.

---

Definition of Done

The organizational structure is complete when every engineering activity, Mission, and operating unit possesses explicit constitutional authority, ownership, and deterministic responsibility.
