Authority

This artifact derives its authority from the PBOS Constitution and the PBOS Charter.

It establishes how authority and responsibility are distributed throughout PBOS.

---

Purpose

Authority shall define what may be decided.

Responsibility shall define what must be accomplished.

PBOS shall clearly distinguish between these two concepts.

---

Authority Model

Founders possess authority over:

vision;

purpose;

mission;

organizational values;

business objectives;

strategic direction.

PBOS possesses authority over:

engineering governance;

mission orchestration;

planning;

dependency management;

validation;

certification;

engineering coordination.

Artificial Intelligence possesses no constitutional authority.

Artificial Intelligence operates only under delegated engineering authority granted by PBOS.

---

Responsibility Model

Every engineering artifact shall possess an identifiable owner.

Every engineering decision shall possess constitutional authority.

Every Mission shall possess accountable stewardship.

Every implementation shall possess traceable responsibility.

Responsibility shall never be anonymous.

---

Delegation

Authority may be delegated.

Responsibility may be assigned.

Accountability remains traceable.

PBOS shall preserve complete evidence of delegation throughout the engineering lifecycle.

---

Conflict Resolution

When conflicts arise, precedence shall be:

Constitution.

Vision.

Charter.

Specifications.

Architecture.

Engineering.

Implementation.

Validation.

Certification.

Release.

Conflicts shall always be resolved in favor of the higher constitutional authority.

---

Validation

PBOS shall continuously verify ownership, delegated authority, accountability, and organizational traceability.

Undefined authority shall fail governance validation.

---

Definition of Done

Authority and responsibility are complete when every engineering activity, artifact, Mission, and decision possesses explicit ownership, constitutional authority, and traceable accountability.
