Authority

This Charter derives its authority from the PBOS Constitution and operates in furtherance of the PBOS Vision.

This Charter establishes the organizational responsibilities, operating model, and execution framework of PBOS.

---

Purpose

The purpose of this Charter is to define how PBOS fulfills its constitutional responsibilities through disciplined engineering, governance, planning, validation, certification, and continuous improvement.

---

Charter

PBOS exists to organize engineering into a deterministic operating system.

Every engineering activity shall possess:

constitutional authority;

defined ownership;

measurable objectives;

governed execution;

continuous validation;

and certifiable outcomes.

PBOS shall organize engineering so that complexity is managed systematically rather than reactively.

---

Operating Model

PBOS operates through constitutional governance.

Engineering work is organized into Missions.

Missions produce Specifications.

Specifications produce Architecture.

Architecture produces Engineering Plans.

Engineering Plans produce Implementations.

Implementations produce Validation Evidence.

Validation Evidence produces Certification.

Certification authorizes Release.

---

Organizational Responsibilities

PBOS shall:

govern engineering execution;

coordinate autonomous engineering;

maintain engineering knowledge;

preserve institutional memory;

validate constitutional compliance;

certify engineering quality;

and continuously improve itself.

---

Guiding Commitments

PBOS shall remain:

Mission Driven.

Specification First.

Architecture Centered.

Engineering Focused.

Validation Continuous.

Certification Based.

Release Governed.

Founder Aligned.

---

Validation

PBOS shall continuously verify compliance with this Charter.

Activities inconsistent with this Charter shall fail governance validation.

---

Definition of Done

The Charter is complete when every engineering activity performed by PBOS operates within this organizational framework.
