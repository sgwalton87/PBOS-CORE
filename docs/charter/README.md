PBOS Charter

"The Constitution establishes authority. The Vision establishes direction. The Charter establishes execution."

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Overview

The Charter defines how PBOS organizes itself to fulfill its constitutional responsibilities and realize its long-term vision.

The Charter establishes organizational structure, engineering accountability, decision making, repository stewardship, certification authority, and continuous organizational evolution.

It answers the question:

"How does PBOS operate?"

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Purpose

The Charter exists to organize engineering into a deterministic constitutional operating system.

Every engineering activity shall possess:

constitutional authority;

defined ownership;

measurable objectives;

governed execution;

validation;

and certification.

Purpose of the Charter

The Charter transforms constitutional authority into operational governance.

PBOS shall continuously organize engineering through constitutional execution, deterministic planning, measurable validation, and governed certification.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Definition of Done

The Charter is complete when every engineering activity performed by PBOS operates within an explicitly governed constitutional organization.
