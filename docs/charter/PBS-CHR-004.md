Authority

This Charter derives its authority from the PBOS Constitution.

It establishes the constitutional lifecycle governing every Mission executed by PBOS.

---

Purpose

Every Mission shall progress through a deterministic lifecycle.

Engineering shall never begin without a Mission.

No Mission shall bypass any required lifecycle stage.

---

Mission Lifecycle

Every Mission shall progress through the following constitutional stages.

Founder Specification.

Mission Definition.

Constitutional Review.

Vision Alignment.

Specification Development.

Architecture.

Engineering Planning.

Implementation.

Validation.

Certification.

Release.

Operations.

Continuous Improvement.

Retirement.

Each stage shall produce measurable engineering evidence.

---

Lifecycle Governance

PBOS shall continuously monitor Mission progression.

No Mission may advance until constitutional requirements for the current stage have been satisfied.

Stages shall fail closed.

Progression shall be deterministic.

---

Lifecycle Evidence

Every Mission stage shall generate:

governing authority;

engineering evidence;

validation evidence;

certification evidence;

institutional knowledge;

and permanent lineage.

PBOS shall preserve this evidence indefinitely.

---

Lifecycle Evolution

Completed Missions remain subject to future evolution.

Engineering improvements shall preserve constitutional lineage.

Mission history shall never be discarded.

---

Validation

PBOS shall validate every Mission transition.

Incomplete stages shall prevent progression.

Unauthorized transitions shall fail governance validation.

---

Definition of Done

The Mission Lifecycle is complete when every Mission progresses deterministically from founder specification through operational stewardship while preserving constitutional authority and complete engineering lineage.
