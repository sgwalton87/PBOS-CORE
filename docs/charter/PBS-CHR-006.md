Authority

This Charter derives its authority from the PBOS Constitution.

It establishes the constitutional stewardship model governing every repository managed by PBOS.

---

Purpose

Repositories preserve engineering knowledge.

PBOS shall treat repositories as governed engineering assets rather than collections of source code.

Every repository shall preserve constitutional authority, engineering lineage, institutional knowledge, and deterministic evolution.

---

Repository Stewardship

Every repository shall possess:

constitutional authority;

defined ownership;

engineering purpose;

artifact lineage;

governed history;

validation evidence;

certification history;

and release history.

Repositories shall remain continuously governable.

---

Repository Responsibilities

PBOS shall:

protect repository integrity;

preserve engineering history;

coordinate repository evolution;

maintain dependency consistency;

prevent constitutional drift;

and continuously certify repository health.

---

Repository Evolution

Repository evolution shall remain deterministic.

Historical engineering evidence shall never be intentionally destroyed.

Engineering improvements shall preserve constitutional lineage.

---

Validation

PBOS shall continuously evaluate repository integrity, governance, traceability, ownership, and constitutional compliance.

Repository violations shall prevent certification.

---

Definition of Done

Repository Stewardship is complete when every repository governed by PBOS continuously preserves constitutional authority, engineering integrity, and institutional knowledge.
