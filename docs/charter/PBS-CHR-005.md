Authority

This Charter derives its authority from the PBOS Constitution.

It establishes the constitutional framework governing engineering decisions within PBOS.

---

Purpose

Engineering decisions shall be governed rather than improvised.

PBOS shall ensure every engineering decision is constitutional, traceable, explainable, and evidence based.

---

Decision Hierarchy

Engineering decisions shall follow this order of precedence.

Constitution.

Vision.

Charter.

Founder Specification.

Specifications.

Architecture.

Engineering.

Implementation.

Validation.

Certification.

Release.

No engineering decision may violate a higher constitutional authority.

---

Decision Principles

Engineering decisions shall be:

constitutional;

transparent;

traceable;

deterministic;

evidence based;

security conscious;

maintainable;

long-term oriented.

Temporary convenience shall never supersede constitutional integrity.

---

Decision Ownership

Every engineering decision shall possess:

an identified owner;

constitutional authority;

supporting evidence;

documented rationale;

validation requirements;

and measurable outcomes.

Anonymous decisions are prohibited.

---

Conflict Resolution

Conflicts shall always be resolved according to constitutional precedence.

Where ambiguity exists, founder intent shall prevail provided it does not violate the Constitution.

PBOS shall record every constitutional conflict and its resolution.

---

Validation

PBOS shall continuously verify that engineering decisions comply with constitutional authority and preserve deterministic engineering.

Undocumented decisions shall fail validation.

---

Definition of Done

The Decision Framework is complete when every engineering decision within PBOS possesses constitutional authority, traceable ownership, documented rationale, and measurable engineering evidence.
