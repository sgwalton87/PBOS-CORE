Authority

This Charter derives its authority from the PBOS Constitution.

It establishes how engineering is organized throughout PBOS.

---

Purpose

Engineering shall operate as a governed constitutional system rather than a collection of independent implementation activities.

Engineering exists to realize founder intent through deterministic execution.

---

Engineering Organization

Engineering shall be organized into constitutional domains.

Every domain possesses:

defined responsibilities;

explicit ownership;

constitutional authority;

validation requirements;

and measurable outcomes.

Engineering domains collaborate through governed interfaces rather than informal dependencies.

---

Engineering Domains

Engineering domains include, but are not limited to:

Mission Engineering.

Specification Engineering.

Architecture Engineering.

Platform Engineering.

Runtime Engineering.

Compiler Engineering.

Validation Engineering.

Certification Engineering.

Release Engineering.

Security Engineering.

Operations Engineering.

Artificial Intelligence Engineering.

Additional domains may be introduced through constitutional evolution.

---

Engineering Responsibilities

Every engineering domain shall:

maintain constitutional compliance;

preserve engineering quality;

maintain deterministic execution;

produce verifiable engineering evidence;

support continuous improvement;

and contribute institutional knowledge.

---

Cross-Domain Collaboration

Engineering domains shall collaborate through governed contracts.

Shared responsibilities shall remain explicitly defined.

Cross-domain dependencies shall remain traceable.

Engineering collaboration shall preserve constitutional authority.

---

Validation

PBOS shall continuously verify engineering ownership, collaboration, dependency integrity, and constitutional compliance across every engineering domain.

Engineering ambiguity shall fail validation.

---

Definition of Done

The engineering organization is complete when every engineering capability operates within a governed constitutional domain possessing explicit ownership, measurable responsibilities, and deterministic interfaces.
