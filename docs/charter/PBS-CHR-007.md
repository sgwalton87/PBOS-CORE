Authority

This Charter derives its authority from the PBOS Constitution.

It establishes every constitutional actor capable of participating within PBOS.

---

Purpose

PBOS governs actors.

Actors perform engineering.

Every engineering action shall be attributable to an identified constitutional actor.

Anonymous engineering is prohibited.

---

Constitutional Actors

PBOS recognizes the following actor classes.

Founder.

Organization.

Executive Leadership.

Engineering Team.

Mission Owner.

Product Owner.

Architect.

Engineer.

Reviewer.

Validator.

Certifier.

Artificial Intelligence Agent.

Planner.

Compiler.

Runtime.

Automation.

Future actor classes approved through constitutional evolution.

---

Responsibilities

Every actor shall possess:

constitutional authority;

defined responsibilities;

documented permissions;

engineering accountability;

traceable activity;

permanent engineering history.

---

Artificial Intelligence

Artificial intelligence is a constitutional actor.

Artificial intelligence operates only through delegated constitutional authority.

Artificial intelligence possesses engineering capability but no independent constitutional authority.

---

Validation

PBOS shall continuously verify actor identity, authority, permissions, ownership, and engineering accountability.

Undefined actors shall fail governance validation.

---

Definition of Done

Constitutional Roles and Actors is complete when every engineering activity can be attributed to a governed constitutional actor possessing explicit authority and accountability.
