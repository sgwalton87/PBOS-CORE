Kernel Observability

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Declaration

Kernel Observability establishes continuous visibility into the operational behavior of PBOS Genesis.

Engineering shall never operate as a black box.

Every constitutional service shall remain observable.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Purpose

The purpose of Kernel Observability is to continuously measure, record, analyze, and explain the operational state of the Constitutional Engineering Operating System.

Observation enables trust.

Observation enables optimization.

Observation enables autonomous engineering.
