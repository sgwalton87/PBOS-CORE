Kernel Resource Management

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Declaration

Kernel Resource Management governs the allocation, protection, optimization, and recovery of every computational, organizational, and engineering resource managed by PBOS Genesis.

Resources are constitutional assets.

Resources shall be governed.

Resources shall be observable.

Resources shall be recoverable.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Purpose

The purpose of Kernel Resource Management is to ensure that every engineering capability receives the resources required to execute while preserving fairness, determinism, security, and constitutional integrity.

Resource management protects organizational continuity.

PBOS Responsibilities

PBOS shall:

allocate resources;

monitor utilization;

detect contention;

recover failed resources;

rebalance workloads;

optimize resource utilization;

maintain resource lineage.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Validation

Resource exhaustion shall never violate constitutional guarantees.

Resource allocation shall remain deterministic.

Definition of Done

Kernel Resource Management is complete when PBOS Genesis continuously allocates, protects, observes, and optimizes constitutional resources while preserving engineering integrity.
