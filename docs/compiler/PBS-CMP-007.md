Compilation Intelligence

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Declaration

Compilation Intelligence is the reasoning capability of the Constitutional
Engineering Compiler.

It continuously improves implementation planning while preserving
constitutional engineering.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Purpose

Compilation Intelligence evaluates engineering history, implementation
evidence, runtime observations, validation results, certification outcomes,
and organizational knowledge to strengthen future compilations.
