Boot Certification

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Declaration

Boot Certification is the constitutional authorization event concluding the initialization lifecycle of PBOS Genesis.

No engineering activity shall execute before successful Boot Certification.

Boot Certification establishes operational trust.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Purpose

The purpose of Boot Certification is to independently verify that PBOS Genesis has initialized correctly, preserved constitutional authority, established deterministic engineering state, and is prepared for governed execution.
