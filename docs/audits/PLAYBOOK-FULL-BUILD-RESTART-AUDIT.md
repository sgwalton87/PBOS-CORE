## Executive finding

The work completed so far built valuable PBOS production machinery and seven
real Scholar-centered cross-domain slices. It did not build or certify the full
Playbook described by the canonical roadmap. The seven journeys were incorrectly
positioned as the aggregate product boundary even though the repository contains
17 distinct operating-system identities, at least 15 role-specific onboarding
pathways, 15 product phases, 58 traced intelligence requirements, and 93 visible
application routes.

PBOS must preserve the valid implementation from those seven slices, but they
are now supplemental cross-domain evidence. They are not the roadmap, queue
authority, completion boundary, or whole-product certification contract.

## Root causes of false progress

- `048-product-journeys` privately modeled seven specifications as the product.
- Static launch planning knew only the same seven journey-to-task mappings.
- The canonical journey manifest could omit every role/OS journey and still be
  structurally acceptable.
- One bounded phase package could complete a phase mission even while its
  manifest declared remaining phase items.
- Route existence and green CI were repeatedly allowed to look like product
  progress without full role/data/authority/browser evidence.
- Documentation, runtime state, and mission evidence were richer than the
  actual certified user experience.

## Enforced restart boundary

- The product graph now requires exactly 17 OS identities and at least 14
  onboarding pathways; the active roadmap contains 15.
- The core acceptance boundary now requires 32 role/OS journeys before adding
  supplemental cross-domain journeys.
- Every OS and onboarding pathway is emitted as an explicit mission dependency.
- Missing acceptance specifications fail closed; PBOS may not silently fall
  back to the seven legacy contracts.
- Product, web-staging, mobile, and final certification remain downstream of
  the complete roadmap graph.

## Dependency-aware build order

1. Canon authority and exact-revision inventory.
2. Identity/authentication foundation.
3. Fifteen role-specific onboarding pathways and record projection.
4. Seventeen role-specific OS experiences and authority boundaries.
5. Remaining phase/domain requirements, routes, states, data, and integrations.
6. Full-roadmap desktop/mobile browser acceptance on one exact revision.
7. Governed web preview, native builds, store readiness, and final certification.

The build is not starting over by deleting working code. It is starting over at
the planning and completion-authority layer so PBOS Genesis builds the real
canonical product and reuses aligned implementation as evidence earns it.
