Evolution Architecture

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Declaration

PBOS Genesis is designed to evolve continuously without sacrificing constitutional integrity.

Evolution is a governed capability.

Evolution shall preserve organizational intent.

Evolution shall preserve engineering knowledge.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Purpose

The Evolution Architecture governs how constitutional systems improve over time.

Every modification shall strengthen organizational intelligence while preserving deterministic engineering and complete traceability.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Evolution Principles

Constitution before change.

Knowledge before optimization.

Backward compatibility whenever practical.

Continuous validation.

Continuous certification.

Institutional knowledge preservation.

Measured improvement.

PBOS Responsibilities

PBOS shall:

measure engineering quality;

identify improvement opportunities;

recommend constitutional evolution;

preserve historical lineage;

predict engineering impact;

support continuous organizational learning.

Validation

Evolution shall never invalidate constitutional authority, organizational identity, or engineering traceability.

Definition of Done

Evolution Architecture is complete when PBOS Genesis continuously improves itself while preserving constitutional governance and engineering continuity.
