Canonical Volume Architecture

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Declaration

A constitutional volume is the primary organizational unit of PBOS Genesis.

Every volume possesses one responsibility.

Every volume exposes one canonical interface.

Every volume remains independently evolvable.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Purpose

The Canonical Volume Architecture establishes the standard organizational structure inherited by every constitutional volume within PBOS Genesis.

Volumes eliminate ambiguity.

Volumes organize engineering knowledge.

Volumes establish deterministic navigation.
