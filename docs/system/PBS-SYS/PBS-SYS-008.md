Extensibility Architecture

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Declaration

PBOS Genesis is an extensible constitutional engineering operating system.

Future capabilities shall extend the operating system without modifying constitutional foundations.

Extensions shall inherit authority.

Extensions shall preserve determinism.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Purpose

The Extensibility Architecture establishes the constitutional framework through which new capabilities, engines, products, organizational domains, and future technologies integrate with PBOS Genesis.

Extensibility enables innovation without architectural fragmentation.

Extension Requirements

Every extension shall define:

constitutional authority;

identity;

purpose;

dependencies;

public interfaces;

validation requirements;

certification requirements;

evolution strategy.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

PBOS Responsibilities

PBOS shall:

discover extensions;

validate compatibility;

integrate extensions;

maintain extension registries;

protect constitutional integrity;

support long-term ecosystem evolution.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Validation

Extensions violating constitutional authority or introducing ambiguous engineering behavior shall fail validation and shall not participate in system execution.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Definition of Done

Extensibility Architecture is complete when PBOS Genesis can continuously incorporate new constitutional capabilities without compromising governance, engineering integrity, or organizational knowledge.
