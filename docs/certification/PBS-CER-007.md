Certification Optimization

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Declaration

Certification Optimization continuously improves constitutional trust while
preserving governance, engineering integrity, and deterministic decision
making.

Optimization shall strengthen trust.

Optimization shall never weaken constitutional authority.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Purpose

Certification Optimization evaluates certification performance, engineering
quality, governance outcomes, repository history, and organizational learning
to improve constitutional trust.

PBOS Responsibilities

PBOS shall:

measure certification quality;

identify optimization opportunities;

evaluate optimization impact;

continuously strengthen constitutional trust.

Definition of Done

Certification Optimization is complete when PBOS Genesis continuously improves

governance.
