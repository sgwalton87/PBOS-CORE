Certification Intelligence

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Declaration

Certification Intelligence is the constitutional reasoning capability that
continuously strengthens engineering trust throughout PBOS Genesis.

Trust shall improve through evidence.

Trust shall never be speculative.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Purpose

Certification Intelligence evaluates certification outcomes, engineering
history, validation evidence, runtime observations, repository evolution,
organizational learning, and governance decisions to improve future
certification.
