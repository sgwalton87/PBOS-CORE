Certification Authority

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Declaration

Certification Authority establishes the constitutional authority governing
engineering trust within PBOS Genesis.

No engineering shall become constitutionally trusted without Certification
Authority.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Purpose

Certification Authority defines who may certify engineering, how trust is
established, how certification decisions are governed, and how constitutional
authority is preserved throughout the engineering lifecycle.

PBOS Responsibilities

PBOS shall:

govern certification authority;

publish certification decisions;

preserve certification evidence;

support constitutional audits;

maintain engineering trust.

Definition of Done

Certification Authority is complete when every constitutional trust decision
is performed under governed authority with complete evidence,
traceability, accountability, and engineering integrity.
