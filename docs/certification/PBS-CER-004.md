Certification Services

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Declaration

Certification Services provide the constitutional trust capabilities of
PBOS Genesis.

Every certification capability shall execute as an independently governed
constitutional service.

Certification Services establish engineering trust through deterministic,
repeatable evaluation.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Purpose

Certification Services evaluate validated engineering against constitutional,
organizational, operational, architectural, and governance requirements to
determine release eligibility.

Certification establishes trust.
