Adaptive Certification

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Declaration

Adaptive Certification enables PBOS Genesis to continuously strengthen
constitutional trust through governed organizational learning.

Certification shall continuously improve.

The Constitution shall remain immutable.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Purpose

Adaptive Certification transforms certification evidence, validation history,
engineering outcomes, governance decisions, runtime intelligence, and
organizational knowledge into progressively stronger constitutional trust.

PBOS Responsibilities

PBOS shall:

learn from certification outcomes;

strengthen certification models;

continuously improve constitutional trust.

constitutional trust while preserving constitutional authority and
engineering integrity.
