Certification Principles

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Declaration

Certification Principles establish the immutable constitutional laws governing
engineering trust.

Certification shall never be influenced by convenience, urgency, or
implementation technology.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Canonical Principles

Constitution Before Certification.

Evidence Before Trust.

Validation Before Certification.

Transparency Before Approval.

Repeatability Before Acceptance.

Explainability Before Trust.

Human Authority Before Autonomous Certification.

Continuous Accountability.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Certification Guarantees

Every certification shall remain:

Independent.

Governed.

Observable.

Recoverable.

Deterministic.

Versioned.

Auditable.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

PBOS Responsibilities

PBOS shall evaluate certification independently of implementation engines and
repository state while preserving constitutional authority.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Definition of Done

Certification Principles are complete when every certification decision
satisfies constitutional engineering governance.
