Certification Lifecycle

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Declaration

The Certification Lifecycle defines the canonical constitutional process for
establishing engineering trust.

Certification shall occur after successful validation and before Release.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Lifecycle Principles

Independent Review.

Deterministic Decisions.

Governed Authority.

Continuous Traceability.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

PBOS Responsibilities

PBOS shall:

coordinate certification;

evaluate engineering evidence;

publish certification decisions;

record certification history;

determine release eligibility.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Definition of Done

The Certification Lifecycle is complete when every validated engineering
artifact progresses through deterministic constitutional trust evaluation
before Release.
