# PBOS Discovery Intelligence Engine

## Purpose

The Discovery Intelligence Engine transforms organizational evidence into a
canonical understanding of an organization.

It is the first intelligence layer of the PBOS Organizational Engineering
Compiler.

Discovery Intelligence SHALL collect, validate, normalize, correlate, and
reason over evidence before any organizational compilation occurs.

The output of this engine SHALL become the authoritative input to the COIR
Compiler.

---

# Responsibilities

• Collect evidence

• Normalize evidence

• Validate provenance

• Fuse multiple evidence sources

• Compute confidence

• Build the Organizational Knowledge Graph

• Produce Organizational Understanding

• Generate Discovery Reports

---

# Constitutional Principles

Discovery SHALL describe reality rather than infer intent.

Every assertion SHALL preserve provenance.

Every confidence score SHALL be explainable.

Every organizational fact SHALL remain traceable to source evidence.

Discovery Intelligence SHALL fail closed.
