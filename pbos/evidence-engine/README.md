# PBOS Evidence Intelligence Compiler

## Purpose

The Evidence Intelligence Compiler provides the evidence foundation
for PBOS Genesis.

Its responsibility is to transform raw information into validated,
traceable, compiler-ready evidence artifacts.

## Role in Genesis

The Evidence Engine sits between discovery and intelligence.

## Core Responsibilities

### Acquisition

Collect information from approved sources.

### Normalization

Transform information into a consistent representation.

### Validation

Verify evidence quality, completeness, and integrity.

### Lineage

Preserve the origin and transformation history of every artifact.

### Reporting

Produce evidence quality reports.

## Constitutional Principles

Evidence SHALL:

- Preserve provenance.
- Maintain lineage.
- Avoid unsupported conclusions.
- Remain auditable.
- Support deterministic compilation.
