# Constitutional Mission Generator

## Purpose

Compile verified organizational capability gaps into constitutionally governed
engineering missions.

The Mission Generator identifies work that MUST exist for an organization to
achieve its certified target state.

It does not invent work.

It discovers constitutionally required work.

---

## Inputs

- Canonical Organization Model
- Constitutional Evidence
- Organizational Capabilities
- Repository State
- Discovery Intelligence

---

## Outputs

- Mission Graph
- Mission Queue
- Planning Evidence

---

## Responsibilities

- Discover capability gaps
- Generate constitutional missions
- Preserve evidence lineage
- Preserve constitutional authority
- Preserve founder intent

---

## Failure Modes

- Missing Organization Model
- Missing Constitutional Evidence
- Unsupported capability
- Ambiguous authority
- Incomplete organizational understanding

---

## Certification

Every mission SHALL be:

- Deterministic
- Explainable
- Traceable
- Independently certifiable
