# Constitutional Prioritization Engine

## Purpose

Determine the single highest-priority eligible mission available for
constitutional execution.

Priority emerges from constitutional reasoning.

Priority is never assigned arbitrarily.

---

## Inputs

- Mission Graph
- Dependency Graph
- Organizational Risk
- Repository State
- Constitutional Evidence

---

## Outputs

- Prioritized Mission Queue
- Selected Mission
- Planning Evidence

---

## Responsibilities

- Rank eligible missions
- Preserve deterministic ordering
- Preserve constitutional authority
- Explain prioritization decisions

---

## Failure Modes

- No eligible missions
- Missing evidence
- Unresolved dependencies
- Repository inconsistency

---

## Certification

Prioritization SHALL be:

- Deterministic
- Explainable
- Repeatable
