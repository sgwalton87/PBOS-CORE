# PBOS Intermediate Representation (PIR)

## Purpose

The PBOS Intermediate Representation (PIR) is the canonical engineering
representation produced by the Organizational Engineering Compiler.

Every compiler stage SHALL consume PIR and produce a refined PIR.

PIR SHALL remain deterministic, versioned, explainable, auditable,
and lineage preserving.

---

## Responsibilities

• Represent organizational structure

• Represent organizational capabilities

• Represent organizational relationships

• Represent organizational constraints

• Represent organizational objectives

• Preserve compiler lineage

• Preserve compiler evidence

• Enable compiler optimization
