## Purpose

The Discovery Gateway is the constitutional entry point into PBOS Genesis.

Every organization SHALL begin with Discovery.

The Gateway determines the organization's execution strategy, initializes a
Discovery Session, and transfers control to the Discovery Orchestrator.

The Discovery Gateway SHALL remain provider-independent and deterministic.
