# PBOS Organization Compiler

> Every organization deserves a canonical representation.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

## Purpose

The Organization Compiler transforms Constitutional Discovery into the
Canonical Organization Intermediate Representation (COIR).

COIR becomes the authoritative representation of an organization throughout
PBOS Genesis.

Every downstream Constitutional Engine SHALL consume COIR.

The Organization Compiler SHALL preserve founder intent, constitutional
authority, evidence lineage, and organizational understanding.
