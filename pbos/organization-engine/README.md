# PBOS Organization Compiler Engine

## Purpose

The Organization Compiler Engine transforms structured knowledge
into organizational operating models.

It represents the transition from:

Knowledge

to:

Organization.

## Position In Genesis

The Organization Engine operates after the Knowledge Compiler.

## Responsibilities

### Organization Modeling

Creates structured representations of organizational systems.

### Capability Modeling

Identifies what an organization can do.

### Role Architecture

Defines responsibilities, ownership, and accountability.

### Workflow Modeling

Defines operational processes and dependencies.

### Operating Structure

Transforms organizational understanding into executable models.

## Constitutional Principles

Organization models SHALL:

- Preserve knowledge lineage.
- Maintain evidence provenance.
- Define accountable ownership.
- Remain explainable.
- Support human governance.
