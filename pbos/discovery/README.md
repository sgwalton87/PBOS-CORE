# PBOS Constitutional Discovery Engine

> "Describe your organization. We'll engineer the platform."

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

## Purpose

The Constitutional Discovery Engine is the executable implementation of the
PBS-DSC Constitutional Discovery Domain.

Its responsibility is to transform incomplete organizational knowledge into
governed constitutional artifacts suitable for autonomous engineering.

The Discovery Engine serves as the front door of PBOS Genesis.

Every organization begins here.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

## Responsibilities

The Discovery Engine shall:

• conduct guided organizational discovery;

• collect constitutional evidence;

• interview founders and stakeholders;

• identify organizational capabilities;

• construct the Organization Constitution;

• construct the Organization Genome;

• construct the Constitutional Knowledge Graph;

• calculate the Organizational Understanding Index (OUI);

• generate Discovery Packages;

• generate Certification Packages;

• determine organizational readiness for autonomous engineering.

## Constitutional Principle

The Discovery Engine SHALL NEVER invent organizational truth.

When organizational understanding is insufficient, the Discovery Engine shall
continue discovery until constitutional confidence reaches the required
certification threshold or a human decision is required.
